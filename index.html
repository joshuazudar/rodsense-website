Rod Sense

Practice fly casting with your phone and get instant feedback on every cast.

Coming soon to Google Play

While you cast

  • Hold your phone like a rod handle and cast. A voice coach calls out problems as they happen: "Don't curve" when a stroke swings around your body, "Stay on plane" when your casting plane jumps.
  • See your rod angle live, where your last forward cast stopped, and how many straight strokes you've made in a row.

After every session

  • A score for how straight your strokes ran and how repeatable your casting was.
  • One clear finding with a drill to fix it, read aloud.
  • Every cast listed, and a drawing of any cast compared with your best of the session.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your casting data stays on your phone unless you choose to share it. Privacy policy
